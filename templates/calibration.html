HOW TO READ IT

Two lines tell the story

Dashed line

This is the target. A 70% predicted chance should line up with a 70% actual win rate.

Green line

This is what happened in the held-out data. The closer it stays to the dashed line, the better the probabilities match past results.

Above the dashed line

Wins happened more often than the model expected. It may be underestimating win chances in that range.

Below the dashed line

Wins happened less often than the model expected. It may be overestimating win chances in that range.

Example

If the model gives lots of match states about a 70% chance, we'd hope about 70% of those states end in a chasing-team win.
